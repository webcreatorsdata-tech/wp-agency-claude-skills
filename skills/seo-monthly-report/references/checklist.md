# SEO Monthly Report: Pre-delivery Checklist

## Data
- Every number in the report comes from data the user provided
- Each number says which source it came from (Search Console, Analytics, rank tracker)
- Month-over-month changes are calculated correctly (percentage and direction)
- Date ranges match between this month and last month
- Missing data is marked "Data not provided", not estimated

## Content
- Summary is 3 to 5 lines and readable without opening the rest
- The 2 or 3 most important findings are called out first
- Wins are tied to the client's goals, not just traffic
- Drops and problems are reported plainly with likely reasons
- Causes are described with appropriate caution ("likely related to")
- No guarantees or promises about future rankings or revenue

## Work log
- Only completed work the user confirmed is listed
- Each item says what was done and on which page or area
- Planned work is clearly separated from completed work

## Next steps
- Actions are prioritized
- Items needing client approval or payment are marked
- Each action connects to a goal or a problem in the report

## Language and format
- Jargon explained in one short phrase on first use
- Tables used for numbers, short paragraphs for explanation
- No private credentials or account details
- Client name, website and month are correct in the header
