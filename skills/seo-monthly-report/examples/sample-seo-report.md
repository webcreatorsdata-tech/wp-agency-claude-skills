# Monthly SEO Report (sample, fictional)

**Client:** Example Business Pvt. Ltd. (not a real client)
**Website:** https://example.com
**Month:** September 2026
**Prepared by:** Web Creators India

## Summary

Search visibility improved this month. More people saw and clicked our listings on Google, mainly because of the two service pages we rewrote in August. Average position slipped slightly for one important search term, and we have a plan to recover it. Enquiries from the website were steady.

## Key numbers

Source: Google Search Console (clicks, impressions, CTR, position) and the enquiry form count provided by the client.

| Metric | September | August | Change |
|--------|-----------|--------|--------|
| Clicks from Google | 1,240 | 1,105 | +12% |
| Impressions (times shown) | 38,500 | 35,200 | +9% |
| CTR (share of impressions that became clicks) | 3.2% | 3.1% | +0.1 pt |
| Average position | 14.8 | 15.6 | Improved by 0.8 |
| Website enquiries | 19 | 19 | No change |

## Work completed

- Rewrote titles and descriptions on 6 service pages
- Added a FAQ section to the main service page
- Fixed 4 broken internal links
- Submitted updated sitemap in Search Console

## Wins

- The "Service A" page gained clicks after its title and description were rewritten. This is likely related to the update, although other factors can also play a part.
- Average position improved across the site.

## Problems and risks

- The page for "Service B" moved down for its main search term. We think a competitor published a stronger page, but we have not confirmed this.
- Two pages show as "Crawled, currently not indexed" in Search Console. They may be too similar to other pages.
